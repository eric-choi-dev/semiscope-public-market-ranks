# Semiscope public market ranks

The bottom-probability model ranks eligible US exchange listings with at least 252 completed trading sessions. It uses the closing price and the simple average of the most recent 14 true ranges (ATR), including the entry day. The close must lie within the smaller of one ATR or 8% of entry above the 20-session closing low, and at least two ATR below the previous 63-session closing high. Outside this zone the score is zero.

Within the zone the model estimates the historical frequency of a joint outcome. Every close during the next 40 trading sessions must stay above or at entry minus the smaller of one ATR or 8% of entry. At least one close must reach a gain of two entry-day ATR, capped at 20% of entry and floored at 5%. The session-40 close must also finish at or above entry. The calibration pools eligible listings and uses only outcomes that matured before the observation date. Insufficient evidence leaves the score unavailable. Each compact packet reconstructs the displayed result and its model version. The dataset also preserves the original accumulation and timing fields for historical comparisons.

The calculation matches the Nasdaq Trader directory date to completed, commit-pinned OHLCV data. If the directory advances before prices, the previous ranking keeps its trading date. Scores and ranks are adapted from [DoltHub stocks](https://www.dolthub.com/repositories/post-no-preference/stocks) and published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The scheduled workflow checks at 07:17 and 15:41 UTC. Each successful publication records its date, digest, coverage, price revision and directory checksum in `last-run.json`. The matching listing directory is archived in `last-directory.json.gz`. Workflow dispatch with `verify_current` reproduces the current release from that directory and its historical price revision.

The workflow retains the eight most recent verified Nasdaq directory dates in `pending-directories/`. When price history arrives a day behind the newest directory, it can publish that completed price date using the matching archived listings. A missing matching directory leaves the previous publication in place.

The one-time `migrate_unified` update to `bottom-probability-2.0` uses the November 23, 2026 cohort and the exact version 5 public data release. `node scripts/refresh-cloud-ranks.mjs --check-packets-file <file>` checks each new probability packet and its tie rank offline. Review the local candidate against the published baseline with `node scripts/refresh-cloud-ranks.mjs --review-unified-candidate <file>`. Dispatch the migration with its reported `expected_content_sha256`. The cloud run independently reproduces the candidate, checks the previous fields and verifies the current sidecar version before publishing. Subsequent runs use the same reviewed model and calibration files. The publishing key is specific to the numerical data site.

The same schedule independently extends the [public daily price history](https://github.com/eric-choi-dev/semiscope-public-market-ranks/tree/history-deltas). It starts from the 4,389-symbol November 20, 2026 release and appends completed sessions from one pinned Dolt revision. Each update checks the preceding day, corporate actions and coverage, and records a content hash. A symbol whose price basis can no longer be verified is omitted from later daily updates. The data branch contains only CC BY-SA numerical files and attribution. Ranking publication and price-history publication can advance independently.
